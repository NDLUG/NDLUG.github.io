## What is LUG?

LUG is the **Notre Dame Linux User's Group**. At our meetings, volunteers from
the community give presentations about cool open source software tools and
concepts. Take a look at the [semester schedule]({{<ref "/#schedule">}}) to get
an idea of topics we will cover during the semester.

You can find a list of this year's LUG officers along with their portfolio
websites in the ["Officers" section on the landing
page]({{<ref "/#officers">}}).

## How to participate

Unlike other clubs, we don't really require people to do any kind of signup
process, pay dues, or participate in mandatory meetings or activities.

We would love for you to attend meetings! You can learn about meeting dates,
locations, and topics by...

- Following the
  [LUG Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0?cid=Y19hMmMzYzQ3YzdiZTc2YjI2YWU0NWZkZGU4OGZhMTllZmVkNmY4MjhkODQ5Njk0NjNlOWY4OWRmZjgwMzcxYjZkQGdyb3VwLmNhbGVuZGFyLmdvb2dsZS5jb20)
- Checking the [semester schedule]({{<ref "/#schedule">}}) on the front page of
  this website
- Signing up for our
  [mailing list](https://groups.google.com/a/nd.edu/g/ndlug-mailing-list)

If you aren't able to attend a meeting but still want to learn about the topic,
please check out the corresponding blog post linked in the
[schedule]({{<ref "/#schedule">}}). These posts (like this one) are meant to be
similar to the in-person meeting, except in prose form. We'll be adding more of
these posts as the semester goes on.

We are always looking for volunteers to present at meetings! If you want to
present on some cool open source thing (or in general, some computer science
thing), please reach out to one of the officers on Matrix, Slack, email, or in
person. If you'd like to learn more about what this process looks like, read our
[contribution guidelines]({{<ref "/club-resources/contribution-guidelines">}}).

## Stay in touch

We'd love for you to join our **Matrix room** to chat with other LUG members!
Check out the ["Get in touch" section on the landing
page]({{<ref "/#get-in-touch">}}) for more information.

We also have a **Minecraft server** hosted at `minecraft3.ndlug.org`.

- The server is configured to only be online at certain times to encourage
  everyone to play together :)
- **Please let us know what times would work best for you on this
  [when2meet](https://www.when2meet.com/?38434226-FRHau) so we can schedule the
  uptime accordingly.**
- The server is behind a **whitelist**. Please email
  [sneisewa@nd.edu](mailto:sneisewa@nd.edu) your username so we can whitelist
  you.
